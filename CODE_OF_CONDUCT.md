# Code of Conduct

This community exists so builders, artists and entrepreneurs can show work, exchange knowledge and get better together.

## Expected

- Be respectful and constructive — critique work, not people.
- Share knowledge generously; credit ideas that are not yours.
- Keep posts on topic and in the right category.
- English and Spanish are both welcome.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Spam, scams or empty self-promotion (a link with no story behind it).
- Sharing other people's private information or unreleased work.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, move or remove posts and, in serious or repeated cases, block accounts. Decisions aim to keep the square useful for the people who show up to build.

By participating you agree to these terms. Let's keep it a place worth showing up to.
