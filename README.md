# SwMusic Community

**Building in public.** This is the open square of [SwMusic](https://sw-music.com) — a music platform with streaming, live radio and a creators program, built independently and shipped step by step.

No hype here: we post what is already live, what we are testing, and what comes next. If you are a builder, an artist or an entrepreneur working on your own thing, this is a good corner of the internet to think out loud with people doing the same.

## What SwMusic is

- 🎧 **Streaming platform** — web at [sw-music.com](https://sw-music.com), plus native iOS and Android apps
- 📻 **Live radio, 24/7** — stations running around the clock
- 🎤 **Creators platform** — independent artists upload and publish their own music
- 🤖 **AutoMix** — our automatic DJ engine: server-planned transitions, beatmatching and SYNC, already mixing live
- 🔊 **Alexa skill** — SwMusic on voice devices

## What's happening now

- **AutoMix** is in its validation phase: live mixing tests every week, including a pilot that feeds a radio station with creators' mixes. Progress reports land in [Discussions](../../discussions) as they happen.
- The home rotates weekly — featured picks and genre rails refresh every Monday.
- First 28 users in, zero ads spent. Organic, one listener at a time.

## Join the conversation

- 📈 **Announcements** — progress reports and releases (the devlog)
- 🔨 **Show and tell** — your turn: show what YOU are building
- 💡 **Ideas** — features, experiments, wild thoughts for the platform
- ❓ **Q&A** — questions about SwMusic, the apps, the radios

## Ground rules

Show your work. Share what you learn. Credit other people's ideas. Keep it respectful — the full code of conduct is [here](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), and how to take part is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
