# Taking part

SwMusic's product code is private — this repository is the community square: progress reports, ideas, feedback, and builders showing their work.

## Ways to contribute

- **Test and report** — try the [web](https://sw-music.com), the iOS or Android apps, or the live radios, and post what you find in Discussions (Q&A or Ideas). Specific beats vague: device, what you did, what happened.
- **Ideas** — feature ideas and experiments go in Discussions → Ideas. The ones we build get credited in the progress report that ships them.
- **Show your work** — building something of your own? Post it in Show and tell. This community grows by people shipping, not by people watching.
- **Words** — corrections, translations and documentation improvements are welcome as pull requests to this repo.

## What happens with good contributions

They get seen. Consistent, high-quality participation is how future SwMusic collaborators are found — not through applications, but through work shown here over time.

## Ground rules

See the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Short version: be respectful, no spam, and no self-promotion without substance — show the work, not just the link.
